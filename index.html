Water Monsters

A fishing game for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Take a small boat out on the great rivers and seas of the world: the Amazon, the Caribbean, the Mississippi, the Congo, Lake Victoria, the Danube, the Yangtze, the Mekong and the Great Barrier Reef. Read the water, find the fish on your finder, and land the monsters that live there, from piranhas to giant catfish, groupers and marlin, to fill your journal.
